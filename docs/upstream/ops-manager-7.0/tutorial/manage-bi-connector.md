# Manage BI Connector in Ops Manager

## Modify a BI Connector Instance

To modify a `BI Connector` instance:

**Step 1: Navigate to the Processes tab for your deployment.**

a. If it is not already displayed, select the organization that
   contains your desired project from the `office` Organizations menu in the
   navigation bar.

#. If it is not already displayed, select your desired project
   from the Projects menu in the navigation bar.

#. Click Processes in the sidebar.

**Step 3: Navigate to your `BI Connector` instance.**

a. Click the cluster card that has the `BI Connector` instance.

#. In the View field in upper-left corner, click 
   the BI Connector option.

**Step 4: Modify the `BI Connector` instance.**

a. Click Modify.

#. Update any settings that you need to change.

**Step 5: Click Apply Changes.**

**Step 6: Click Review & Deploy to review your changes.**

Ops Manager displays your proposed changes.

a. If you are satisfied, click Confirm & Deploy.
b. If you want to make further configuration changes, 
   click Cancel. Click Modify for the
   cluster to make additional changes.

## Terminate a BI Connector Instance

To shut down a `BI Connector` instance:

**Step 1: Navigate to the Processes tab for your deployment.**

a. If it is not already displayed, select the organization that
   contains your desired project from the `office` Organizations menu in the
   navigation bar.

#. If it is not already displayed, select your desired project
   from the Projects menu in the navigation bar.

#. Click Processes in the sidebar.

**Step 2: Click list.**

**Step 3: Navigate to your `BI Connector` instance.**

a. Click the cluster card that has the `BI Connector` instance.

#. In the View field in upper-left corner, click 
   the BI Connector option.

**Step 4: Terminate the `BI Connector` instance.**

In the card for that `BI Connector` instance, click the ellipsis-h button and select Terminate.

**Step 5: **

In the Terminate Cluster: modal:

a. Type the cluster name in the box.
b. Click Terminate.

**Step 6: Click Review & Deploy to review your changes.**

Ops Manager displays your proposed changes.

a. If you are satisfied, click Confirm & Deploy.
b. If you want to make further configuration changes, 
   click Cancel. Click Modify for the
   cluster to make additional changes.
