# Monitor Your Deployments

Ops Manager provides built-in tools, alerts, charts, integrations, and 
logs to help you monitor your cluster. Ops Manager provides the following 
ways to monitor your deployments and improve performance.

## Slow Queries

To optimize your query performance, review the best 
practices for query performance. You can also 
analyze slow queries and troubleshoot 
slow operations executed on your clusters.

Use the following built-in tools:

- Monitor and improve slow queries with 
  the Performance Advisor.
   
- Monitor query performance with the 
  Profiler.

## Schema Design

To optimize your schema design, review our frequently used schema 
design patterns. You can also improve 
your schema. Improve your schema based on 
recommendations from the Performance Advisor and the Data Explorer.

## Alerts

To trigger alerts based on alert conditions 
and to help ensure deployment performance, 
configure alerts and resolve them promptly. You can 
configure alerts based on specific conditions for your 
databases, users, accounts, and more. When you resolve alerts, you 
should fix the immediate problem, implement a long-term solution, and 
monitor your progress.

## Deployment Metrics

To monitor your deployment performance, view 
deployment metrics. View historical 
throughput, performance, and utilization metrics for your databases. To 
learn more, review the available metrics.

## Third-Party Integrations

To receive MongoDB Atlas alerts in various 
external monitoring services, 
integrate with third-party monitoring services.

## MongoDB Logs

To view log messages and system event audit messages, view and download your MongoDB logs.

## Diagnostic Archives

To debug and troubleshoot issues, 
locate and download the diagnostic archives.
